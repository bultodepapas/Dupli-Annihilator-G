# Dupli-Annihilator-G v2.9.1

## Executive Summary

`v2.9.1` improves the desktop runtime experience with a new **focus mode** while the main deduplication job is running.

The screen now shifts attention to the operational controls and live telemetry: the background workspace becomes softly blurred and non-interactive, while the output panel, header status, and telemetry dock remain sharp, elevated, and readable.

---

## Feature: Running Focus Mode

### What changed

During the main `RUN` flow, the desktop UI now enters a cinematic glass-style focus state:

- The main workspace content is blurred, dimmed, and interaction-blocked.
- The **Output** panel becomes the primary sharp control surface.
- The live telemetry bar stays clear and visually elevated as a persistent operations dock.
- The top bar remains readable without competing with progress details.

### Why it matters

This makes long-running jobs feel more deliberate and easier to track:

- users can immediately see what the app is doing,
- progress and cancel controls stay visually prioritized,
- accidental interaction with background settings is prevented while a job is active.

---

## UX Details

- Focus mode applies only to the primary deduplication run.
- Summary/report screens are unchanged.
- Responsive behavior was adjusted so the focused output panel remains visible on narrower windows.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected by disabling the added transitions where appropriate.
